Lumify

A calm space for understanding your skin - made simple and supportive.

UX Research AI Integration Mobile App

The Vision

Lumify is designed to support individuals who feel overwhelmed by skincare routines.

Insight 01

70% of users don't know their own skin type accurately.

The Outcome

By providing clear ingredient insights, users reported a 40% increase in confidence regarding their product choices.

Our UX enhancements are tailored to three key personas: the DTC Customer, the Affiliate Partner, and the Third-Party Retailer. Each faces unique friction points that our design aims to resolve.
