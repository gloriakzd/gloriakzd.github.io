Lumify

A calm space for understanding your skin - made simple and supportive.

UX Research AI Integration Mobile App

Phase 01 · The Vision

A Calm Space in a Complex World.

The Problem

Skincare is a stressful maze of inconsistent advice. For many, finding a routine feels overwhelming and isolating—a cycle of trial and error.

🔍
The Objective

To be an empathetic guide. We simplify complexity with credible, AI-personalized insights to turn skincare into a supportive ritual.

🧴
Target Audience
Beginners Sensitive Skin Consistency Seekers

Young individuals seeking a judgement-free community and predictable, calm guidance.

Key Insight
70%

of users don't know their own skin type accurately, leading to improper product choices.

We designed Lumify around real-life friction. By looking through the eyes of our three core user personas, we uncovered the specific moments where a little extra clarity and support go a long way.
