Myro is a modern fragrance and essential oil brand that helps people feel grounded, refreshed, and connected through scent-driven routines.

The brand focuses on minimalism, intentional living, and mood-based aromas, creating products that turn everyday care into a sensory experience.

Primary Sans-Serif

The choice of Neue Haas Grotesk provides a sleek, minimal precision that keeps the Myro brand feeling clean and elevated.

Paired with a high-contrast serif, they shape a visual voice that is calm, intentional, and effortlessly refined—mirroring the sensory experience of the fragrance itself.

Secondary Accent Serif

Aabak delivers personality with its warm, organic letterforms, making headlines feel intimate and human.

This pairing softens the brand's minimal edge, adding a layer of approachability that invites the user to slow down.

01 / THE PROBLEM

Fragmented Care

Users often feel overwhelmed by conflicting skincare advice and complex ingredient lists, leading to a lack of confidence in their daily routines.

02 / THE SOLUTION

Guided Clarity

Lumify simplifies the journey by providing a centralized, supportive space that translates complex data into actionable, personalized skin insights.

Concept Description

The Lumify interface is built on a foundation of cognitive ease. By utilizing soft gradients, ample whitespace, and intuitive navigation, the platform reduces the "medical anxiety" often associated with clinical skincare.

Every interaction is designed to feel like a supportive conversation, ensuring that users stay grounded and informed as they track their skin's health over time.
